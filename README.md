## 🗺️ Roadmap & Structure

The repository is organized into four distinct phases, mirroring my learning path:

### [Phase 1: Foundations](Phase1/)
*Quantum Mechanics, Linear Algebra, Qiskit Basics*
- **Focus:** Mastering the math and physics behind quantum computing.
- **Tools:** Python, NumPy, Qiskit.

### [Phase 2: Cryptography & PQC](Phase2/)
*Classical Crypto, Lattice-based Cryptography, PQC Algorithms*
- **Focus:** Understanding classical security and transitioning to post-quantum standards (Kyber, Dilithium).
- **Tools:** OpenSSL, liboqs.

### [Phase 3: Quantum Communication](Phase3/)
*QKD Protocols (BB84, E91), Entanglement, Teleportation*
- **Focus:** Simulating and implementing quantum communication networks.
- **Tools:** Qiskit, QuTiP.

### [Phase 4: Advanced Topics](Phase4/)
*Quantum Machine Learning (QML), Compiler Optimization, Error Correction*
- **Focus:** Cutting-edge research and optimization techniques.
- **Tools:** PennyLane, Qiskit Aer.

### [Projects](Projects/)
*Flagship Implementations & Capstone Projects*
- Major projects demonstrating end-to-end quantum security solutions.

### [Docs](Docs/)
